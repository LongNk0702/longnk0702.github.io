I am a final-year B.Eng. student in Automation and Informatics at Vietnam National University, Hanoi. My research interests lie at the intersection of robotics, control systems, wireless power transfer, and AI-enabled autonomous systems.

My current research focuses on autonomous underwater vehicles,6-DOF delta robot, planar wireless power transfer system, and prediction model for sensing data. I am currently supervised by Dr. Le Xuan Hai on my undergraduate research project.

I have also gained research experience in different international research environments. At Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, I worked on underwater wireless power transfer under the supervision of Asst. Prof. Yun Yang. At National Chung Cheng University, Taiwan, I worked on smart manufacturing and data-driven modeling under the supervision of Prof. Her-Terng Yau.

These experiences have shaped my interest in connecting theoretical control methods, data-driven modeling, energy systems, and practical engineering challenges. As a life-long learner, I enjoy exploring new ideas, learning from different research environments, and connecting with people from diverse academic and cultural backgrounds.
