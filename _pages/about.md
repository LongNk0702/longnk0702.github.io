I am a final-year B.Eng. student in Automation and Informatics at Vietnam National University, Hanoi. My research interests lie at the intersection of robotics, control systems, wireless power transfer, and AI-enabled autonomous systems.

My current research focuses on autonomous underwater vehicles,6-DOF delta robot, planar wireless power transfer system, and prediction model for sensing data. I am currently supervised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=PXS0BHMAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dr. Le Xuan Hai</a> on my undergraduate research project.

I have also gained research experience in different international research environments. At Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, I worked on underwater wireless power transfer under the supervision of <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=jQDI8o0AAAAJ">Asst. Prof. Yun Yang</a>. At National Chung Cheng University, Taiwan, I worked on smart manufacturing and data-driven modeling under the supervision of <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=_ILk_K0AAAAJ">Prof. Her-Terng Yau</a>.

These experiences have shaped my interest in connecting theoretical control methods, data-driven modeling, energy systems, and practical engineering challenges. As a life-long learner, I enjoy exploring new ideas, learning from different research environments, and connecting with people from diverse academic and cultural backgrounds.
