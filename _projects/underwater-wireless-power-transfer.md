## Overview

This research project investigates a relay-coil-enhanced spatial wireless power transfer (WPT) system for charging autonomous underwater vehicles. The goal is to improve energy autonomy while reducing the alignment constraints of conventional underwater charging systems.

## My contributions

- Modeled the system's equivalent circuit and analyzed power-transfer efficiency under different loads and operating frequencies.
- Studied coil configurations and relay-coil placement for spatial underwater power transfer.
- Developed mechanical integration concepts with attention to hydrodynamic efficiency and practical AUV charging.
- Explored communication improvements between the underwater transmitter and receiver.

## Outcome

This work was completed during the 2026 NTU Global Connect Fellowship at the Water & Energy Research Lab and received the **Most Popular Poster Award** at the final research presentation.
