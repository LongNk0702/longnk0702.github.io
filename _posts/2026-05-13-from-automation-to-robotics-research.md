My academic journey began in Automation and Informatics, where I was first exposed to control systems, embedded systems, mechanical design, and industrial automation. Over time, I became increasingly interested in robotics because it brings together many engineering disciplines into one intelligent physical system.

Through projects on Delta robot control, YOLOv5-based PPE detection, AGV warehouse simulation, smart manufacturing, and underwater wireless power transfer, I gradually shaped my research interest around autonomous robotic systems.

Today, my long-term goal is to develop robotic technologies that can operate reliably in real-world environments. I am especially interested in combining intelligent control, wireless energy transfer, mechanical design, and AI to build long-endurance autonomous systems.
