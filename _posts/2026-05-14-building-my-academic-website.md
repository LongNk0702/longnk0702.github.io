I started building this academic website as a place to organize my research journey, projects, publications, and academic experiences.

For students and early-stage researchers, a personal website is more than an online CV. It is a living portfolio that reflects research interests, technical growth, and long-term academic direction. It also helps communicate research work more clearly to potential supervisors, collaborators, and scholarship committees.

Through this website, I hope to document my progress in robotics, control, wireless power transfer, and autonomous systems while gradually building a stronger research identity.
